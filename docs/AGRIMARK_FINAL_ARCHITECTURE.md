# AgriMark Final Architecture — Unified National Agricultural OS (Stages 1–30)

## Overview
AgriMark is a production-grade, multi-tenant National Agricultural Intelligence, Commerce, Operations, and Decision Support Platform.

## Unified Layer Stack
1. **DATA**: Canonical Schemas, Lineage, Multilingual Normalization (Stage 23)
2. **IDENTITY**: RBAC, Farmer & FPO Verifications (Stage 1-8)
3. **CONSENT**: Purpose-based Policy Engine & Selective Disclosure (Stage 23)
4. **KNOWLEDGE**: National Research Graph, ICAR & University Package of Practices (Stage 19)
5. **MODELS**: ML/MLOps Engine & Sandbox Innovation Workspace (Stage 24)
6. **AI AGENTS**: Multi-Agent Supervisor & Specialist Swarms (Stage 22, 30)
7. **DECISION INTELLIGENCE**: Standardized Decision Cards (Question, Why, Evidence, Confidence, Risk, Next Steps) (Stage 30)
8. **MARKETPLACE**: Spot Mandis, FPO Pooling & Export Corridors (Stage 9, 29)
9. **FINANCE & INSURANCE**: Credit Exposure & Parametric Claim Evidence (Stage 28)
10. **LOGISTICS & COLD CHAIN**: Multimodal Reefer Route Optimization (Stage 27)
11. **PROCESSING**: Milling & Dehydration Capacity Matcher (Stage 27)
12. **CLIMATE & RESILIENCE**: Disaster Mode Lifecycle & Risk Engine (Stage 29)
13. **BIOLOGY & SEEDS**: Seed Authenticity QR & GxE Stability Analysis (Stage 26)
14. **PHYSICAL OPERATIONS**: Governed Physical AI & Telemetry (Stage 21)
15. **OUTCOME MEASUREMENT**: Causal Diff-in-Diff Impact Evaluation (Stage 25)
16. **CONTINUOUS LEARNING**: Model Economic Champion Selection & Trust Cards (Stage 22, 25)
