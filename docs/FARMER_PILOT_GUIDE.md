# AgriMark Farmer Pilot Guide (உழவர் பயன்பாட்டு கையேடு)

## 1. Getting Started / கணக்கு தொடங்குதல்
1. Navigate to [AgriMark Marketplace](https://agrimark-six.vercel.app/).
2. Select your language (English, தமிழ், हिन्दी, etc.) from the navbar menu.
3. Click **Register Account** and select **Farmer**.
4. Complete your onboarding profile with your Village, District, and State.

## 2. Managing Farms & Crops / பண்ணை மற்றும் பயிர் மேலாண்மை
1. **Add Farm**: Enter farm name, total area (acres), soil type, and irrigation source.
2. **Add Crop Cultivation**: Link your farm to a specific crop (e.g., Erode Salem Turmeric) and specify sowing date and expected harvest date.
3. **Record Field Observations**: Log crop health scores (1-10), pest/disease pressure, and agronomy notes.

## 3. Harvest Logs & Selling Produce Lots / அறுவடை மற்றும் விற்பனை
1. **Record Harvest**: Enter total harvested quantity (KG), quality grade (Grade A, Grade B, Export Quality), and packaging type. A unique **Produce Passport Trace Code** (e.g. `TRC-TURMERIC-2026-001`) is generated automatically.
2. **Create Listing**: Click **Sell Produce Lot**, set asking price (₹/KG), and set minimum order quantity.
3. **Order Management**: Track buyer orders in real-time. When a buyer places an order, the system automatically reserves the inventory.
