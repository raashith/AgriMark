# AgriMark Greenhouse Gas Emissions Methodology

## Framework
AgriMark implements IPCC 2019 Refinement (Tier 2) emission estimation methodologies for agricultural operations.

## Activity Sources Tracked
- Diesel fuel consumption (liter)
- Grid electricity consumption (kWh)
- Synthetic nitrogen fertilizer application (kg N)
- Irrigation pumping volume (m3)
- Logistics & transport (tonne-km)
- Cold storage operations (tonne-day)
- Processing operations (kg)

## Provenance & Claims Strictness
1. **Metadata Mandatory**: Every emission estimate requires factor source, factor version, calculation method, and measurement period.
2. **No Carbon Credit Claims**: Estimates serve as internal operational baselines. AgriMark explicitly does NOT claim unverified carbon offsets or tradable carbon credits.
