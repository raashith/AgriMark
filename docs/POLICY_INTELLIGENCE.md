# AgriMark Policy & Scheme Governance Intelligence OS

## Overview
The Policy Intelligence OS provides evidence-backed information on central & state agricultural schemes, rule-driven eligibility evaluations, document readiness tracking, deadline alerts, policy change detection, compliance checklists, and source trust verification.

## Governance Boundaries
1. **Not a Government Portal**: AgriMark is an independent intelligence and decision-support platform. It does NOT claim government authorization, official endorsement, guaranteed eligibility, guaranteed application approval, or automatic legal submission.
2. **Non-Automated Submissions**: Submissions to government portals are never performed automatically. Applications require manual submission via official government channels (e.g. e-Sevai, AED district portals, PM-KISAN portal).
3. **Legal Disclaimer**: Compliance checklists serve decision-support purposes and do not constitute formal legal advice or legal certification.
