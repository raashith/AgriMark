# AgriMark Water Intelligence & Optimization

## Overview
Water Intelligence combines sensor telemetry, crop evapotranspiration models (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith), and soil moisture sensors to calculate water use, irrigation efficiency, and potential savings.

## Key Principles
1. **Measured vs. Estimated**: Water savings are never claimed as exact unless directly measured via flow meters or sensor telemetry.
2. **Irrigation Optimization**: Recommendations provide recommended timing, quantity, frequency, soil evidence, weather evidence, and maximum recommended limit.
3. **Safety Layer**: All physical irrigation actuator commands remain behind Phase 15 human authorization safety gates.
