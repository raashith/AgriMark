# AgriMark Climate Finance Foundation

## Purpose
Provides decision-support evaluations for climate-resilient agricultural investments:
- Climate-resilient infrastructure
- Water-saving technology (drip micro-irrigation)
- Renewable energy transitions (solar pumping)
- Risk mitigation & parametric index insurance

## Autonomy Limit
All financial proposals are decision-support outputs only. AgriMark performs NO autonomous financial approvals or binding loan/subsidy commitments.
