# AgriMark Evidence Engine & Farmer Knowledge Translation

## Evidence Tiers
1. `PRIMARY_RESEARCH` (Randomized controlled trial / peer-reviewed paper)
2. `META_ANALYSIS` (Statistical synthesis across studies)
3. `SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW` (Protocol-based review)
4. `OFFICIAL_RESEARCH` (ICAR / Agricultural University extension package)
5. `EXPERT_GUIDANCE` (Certified agronomist advice)
6. `OBSERVATIONAL` (Field telemetry)
7. `USER_REPORTED` (Farmer community submission)
8. `AI_GENERATED` (Synthesized draft — cannot independently validate claims)

## Farmer Explanation Model
Translates technical findings into:
- What researchers found
- Why it matters
- Where it applies
- Confidence & limitations
- Relation to farmer's field context
