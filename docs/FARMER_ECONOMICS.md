# AgriMark Farmer Economic Ledger

## Cost & Revenue Abstraction
Tracks production cost, input cost, labor cost, irrigation, transport, storage, platform fees, and gross sale revenue.

## Net Realization Formula
$$\text{Net Realization} = \text{Revenue} - \sum \text{Expenses}$$

Output includes explicit disclaimers that values represent internal economic models, not audited financial statements.
