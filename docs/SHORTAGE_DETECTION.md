# AgriMark Shortage Detection & Redistribution Intelligence

## Multi-Factor Shortage Condition
Shortage risks are calculated by evaluating:
- Available local supply (MT)
- Buffer inventory (MT)
- Inbound logistics pipelines (MT)
- Expected regional demand (MT)

## Key Constraint
**No Single Price Spike Override**: AgriMark strictly prohibits inferring a national or regional food shortage based solely on a single price spike or localized market speculation.
