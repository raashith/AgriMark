# AgriMark Compliance Intelligence & Checklists

## Covered Categories
- Food handling & FSSAI standards
- Quality certification (Agmark)
- Storage facility regulations
- Transport & transit documentation
- Produce traceability & packaging
- Agricultural inputs (fertilizer & pesticide licensing)
- Organic claims (NPOP)
- Export documentation (APEDA)
- Environmental requirements

## Checklist Workflow
Farmer-facing compliance items advance through statuses:
`OPEN` → `IN_PROGRESS` → `READY` (or `EXPIRED` / `NOT_APPLICABLE`).
