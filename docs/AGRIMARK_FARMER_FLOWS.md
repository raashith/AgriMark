# AgriMark Unified Farmer Experience & Workflow Guides

## 1. Unified Farmer Experience
AgriMark provides one cohesive farmer experience:
- **One Account**: Unified sign-in and profile management.
- **One Operating Center**: `/farmer/operating-center`
- **One Action Center**: `/farmer/actions`
- **One Marketplace & Assistant**: Seamless integration of inputs, sales, schemes, and AI guidance.

---

## 2. End-to-End Workflow Stages
1. **Farm & Crop Setup**: Farm boundary creation, soil sample analysis, crop selection.
2. **Growth & Intelligence**: Real-time GDD tracking, disease alerts, pulse drip irrigation recommendations.
3. **Scheme Discovery**: Scheme eligibility evaluation & automated document readiness check.
4. **Harvest & Sale**: Quality verification, direct buyer matching, logistics booking, shipment telemetry.
5. **Settlement & Outcome**: Escrow payout, farmer outcome calculation, digital twin update.
