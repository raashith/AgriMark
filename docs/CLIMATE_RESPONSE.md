# AgriMark Climate Event Response Workflows

## Workflow Progression
1. **Climate Hazard Signal**: Weather forecast or telemetry triggers alert level (INFO, WATCH, WARNING, SEVERE, CRITICAL).
2. **Impacted Farm Identification**: Geographic bounding identifies affected farms and fields.
3. **Advisory Generation**: Dispatches farm-specific advisory and action recommendations.
4. **FPO & Network Notification**: Notifies local FPOs and regional logistics for resource coordination.
5. **Human Approval Gate**: Physical actuator triggers remain gated until human confirmation (Phase 15 safety model).
6. **Outcome Tracking**: Evaluates field yield and economic resilience post-event.
