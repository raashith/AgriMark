# Agricultural Digital Twin Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary
The **AgriMark Digital Twin OS** provides a national-scale computational representation of farms, crops, soil, water, weather, infrastructure, markets, supply, and policy systems.

The digital twin functions as a decision-support system to explore possible futures under climate shocks, market disruptions, and policy interventions. It does not provide guaranteed physical predictions.

---

## 2. Supported Systems & Capabilities
- **Current-State Representation**: Real-time operational snapshot of national agricultural assets.
- **Historical Reconstruction**: Versioned time-series state replay without overwriting historical records.
- **Scenario Simulation**: Drought, flood, heatwave, pest outbreak, and logistics shock modeling.
- **What-If Analysis**: Variable manipulation across acreage, rainfall, water allocation, and crop choices.
- **Risk Forecasting**: District-level water deficits, yield vulnerabilities, and supply bottlenecks.
- **Intervention Comparison**: Multi-variable evaluation of mitigation strategies (storage expansion, irrigation, rerouting).
- **Resilience Planning**: Long-term climate adaptation strategies.
