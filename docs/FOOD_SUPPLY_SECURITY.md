# AgriMark National Food & Agricultural Supply Security OS Architecture

## Overview
The National Food Supply Security OS provides evidence-backed monitoring, shortage detection, bottleneck analysis, trade dependency metrics, and resilience planning across national agricultural supply networks.

## Core Governance Principles
1. **Decision Support & Planning**: AgriMark serves as an intelligence and decision-support operating system. It does NOT claim to control government reserves, public buffer stocks, customs policies, or public food distribution.
2. **Data Type Explicit**: Every metric clearly demarcates `OBSERVED`, `ESTIMATED`, `FORECAST`, `SIMULATION`, or `RECOMMENDATION`.
3. **Critical Infrastructure Security**: Coordinates and granular details of sensitive storage nodes, defense reserves, and critical hubs are masked in public endpoints.
