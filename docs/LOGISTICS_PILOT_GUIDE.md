# AgriMark Logistics Pilot Guide

## 1. Overview
The Logistics Portal enables transport partners and fleet drivers to accept pickup requests, record transit milestones, and submit location updates for confirmed marketplace orders.

## 2. Delivery Workflow
1. Navigate to `/logistics/deliveries`.
2. View available shipment jobs assigned to your region.
3. Click **Accept Job** to transition order state to `confirmed`.
4. Update pickup status (`ready_for_pickup` → `in_transit`).
5. Submit GPS coordinates or location notes during transit.
6. Upon arrival at the buyer facility, confirm delivery to complete the order lifecycle (`delivered` → `completed`).
