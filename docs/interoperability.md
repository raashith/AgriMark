# Interoperability Fabric & Connectors

## 1. Global Agricultural Identifiers
Interoperable reference keys (`farmer_ref`, `farm_ref`, `parcel_ref`, `crop_ref`, `lot_ref`, `market_ref`, `device_ref`) map between internal IDs and authorized external IDs without inventing official government identifiers.

## 2. Multilingual Semantic Registry
`AgriculturalSemanticRegistry` standardizes crop names, varieties, commodities, soil terms, pests, and measurement units across English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, and Punjabi.

## 3. External Connectors
Connector states are tracked explicitly: `CONNECTED`, `CONFIGURED`, `PENDING_AUTHORIZATION`, `SIMULATED`, `UNAVAILABLE`.
