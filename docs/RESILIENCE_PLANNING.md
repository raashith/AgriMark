# AgriMark Resilience Planning & Critical Infrastructure Protection

## Candidate Responses
Generates evidence-backed resilience recommendations:
- Alternative sourcing
- Regional redistribution
- Temporary emergency storage
- Processing transshipment rerouting
- Logistics corridor rerouting

## Governance & Infrastructure Protection
1. **Human Approval**: Consequential recommendations require human authorization.
2. **Critical Node Privacy**: Critical infrastructure nodes (defense storage, major transport hubs) are protected with masked geographic coordinates.
