# AgriMark Storage & Post-Harvest Loss Intelligence

## Storage Tracking
Tracks capacity, available space, temperature/humidity specs, inventory age, and storage quality across dry warehouses, cold storage hubs, silos, and deep freezes.

## Post-Harvest Loss Standards
Loss estimates (harvest, storage, transport, processing) must specify measurement source and accredited methodology (e.g. ICAR CIPHET methodology). Fabrication of loss percentages is strictly forbidden.
