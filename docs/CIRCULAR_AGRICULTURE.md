# AgriMark Circular Agriculture & Waste Marketplace

## Waste Streams
- Crop residue
- Food waste & post-harvest loss
- Packaging materials
- Organic waste
- Livestock manure
- Processing byproducts

## Reuse Pathways
- Composting
- Bio-input formulation
- Animal feed (where biologically safe)
- Bioenergy / Biomass pelletization
- Material recycling
- Direct soil amendment

## Safety Protocol
Biological safety rules strictly prevent unsafe reuse pathways (e.g. raw manure to animal feed, plastic packaging to compost).
