# AgriMark Soil Health Framework

## Overview
The Soil Health module tracks soil chemical, physical, and biological indicators based on accredited lab testing and farmer observations.

## Rules & Constraints
1. **No Value Inference**: Laboratory parameters (such as EC/salinity, NPK, organic carbon) are marked `UNTESTED` when no laboratory measurement exists. Missing values are never fabricated or inferred.
2. **Regenerative Agriculture**: Tracks practices such as cover cropping, crop rotation, reduced tillage, organic amendments, mulching, intercropping, and agroforestry with dates and evidence.
3. **Credit Awarding**: Environmental credits or financial rewards are NOT awarded automatically upon logging.
