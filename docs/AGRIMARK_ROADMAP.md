# AgriMark 30-Stage Platform Architecture Roadmap

This document outlines the complete 30-stage architectural progression of AgriMark.

## Stages 1–30 Roadmap Summary

| Stage Range | Focus Area | Core Features | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stages 1–5** | Foundational Commerce & Identity | User registration, RBAC, Farm management, Crop tracking, Harvest logging. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stages 6–10** | Marketplace & Intelligence | Produce lots, Quality grading, Listings, Order matching, Settlement, Basic ML price forecasts. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stages 11–15** | FPO Aggregation & Logistics | FPO member management, Bulk demand aggregation, Storage directory, Logistics tracking. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stages 16–20** | Digital Twin & Physical AI OS | Sensor telemetry, Digital Twin farm simulation, Drone/machinery integration interfaces. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stages 21–22** | Physical AI & Trust/Safety OS | Controlled physical execution, AI trust/safety guardrails, Model bias/degradation monitoring. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 23** | Data Commons & Interoperability | Data provider/consumer registry, Data contracts, Selective disclosure, Purpose consent rules. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 24** | Innovation Sandbox | Synthetic dataset generator, Developer API portal, Compute simulation sandbox. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 25** | Farmer Outcomes & AI Impact | Economic profile tracking, Baseline vs. intervention evaluation, Impact attribution. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 26** | Seed & Genetic Intelligence | Germplasm registry, Tamil trait ontology, GxE stability, QR seed authenticity scanner. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 27** | Logistics & Cold Storage OS | Cold facility directory, Reefer 4°C telemetry, Storage-vs-sell economic trade-off optimizer. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 28** | Finance & Allied Agriculture | Non-guaranteed credit risk scoring (decision support), Livestock/dairy/fisheries registry. | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 29** | Global Trade & Disaster Mode | Export landed cost, Digital Product Passports, Circular waste flows, Disaster Mode lifecycle (`DETECTED` -> `CLOSED`). | **IMPLEMENTED** |
| **Stage 30** | Unified Decision Engine | Standardized Decision Cards (Question, Recommendation, Why, Evidence, Confidence, Risk, Next Steps). | **IMPLEMENTED** |

---

> [!NOTE]
> Architecture Stage 30 is the final planned architecture layer. Stage 31 will **NOT** be created. Focus remains strictly on production hardening and deployment.
