# AgriMark Operational Controls & Control Plane

## 1. National Control Plane
The **National Control Plane** (`/control-plane`) allows authorized administrators and extension departments to monitor real-time food supply security, crop production, market conditions, water balances, logistics bottlenecks, and system health.

- **API p95 Latency Target**: < 200 ms
- **Error Rate Target**: < 0.05%
