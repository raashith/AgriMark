# AgriMark Trade Intelligence Foundation

## Import Dependency & Export Pressure
- **Import Dependency Ratio**: Calculated as `Imports / Domestic Demand`. Requires empirical trade observation data.
- **Export Pressure**: Tracks export volumes against domestic availability buffer thresholds.

## Policy Boundaries
Trade intelligence outputs provide decision support. AgriMark does NOT make official customs claims or recommend export bans or tariff restrictions.
