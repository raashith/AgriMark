# AgriMark Non-Production Scenario Simulation

## Simulation Capabilities
Models hypothetical shocks including:
- 10% and 25% national production declines
- Major flood and drought events
- Import interruptions and export surges
- Warehouse outages and transport disruptions

## Mandatory Tagging
All simulation results are explicitly tagged with `data_origin = 'SIMULATION'`. Simulation outputs are strictly non-production planning models and must never be presented as empirical facts or official forecasts.
