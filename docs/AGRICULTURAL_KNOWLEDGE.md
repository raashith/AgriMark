# AgriMark National Agricultural Knowledge & Research Intelligence OS

## Overview
The Agricultural Knowledge OS connects farmers, FPOs, researchers, universities, ICAR-style institutions, datasets, research papers, field trials, and disease/soil knowledge with strict evidence provenance.

## Core Governance Principles
1. **Not a Scientific Authority**: AgriMark is an evidence-backed intelligence layer. It does NOT claim independent scientific authority or invent research findings, experimental outcomes, citations, or agronomic guarantees.
2. **Evidence Lineage**: Every scientific claim must specify evidence type (`PRIMARY_RESEARCH`, `META_ANALYSIS`, `SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW`, etc.), source URL, DOI, publication date, methodology, and limitations.
3. **No AI Invention**: `AI_GENERATED` content cannot independently validate scientific claims. All AI-generated experiment structures are explicitly labeled `AI_DRAFT`.
